2.2 Theory: ddrive¶

The next two packages, 2.3 ddrive and 2.4 ddrive_ik (plus the services/actions layered on top in 2.5 ddrive_ik_msgs), are ROS 2 wrappers around math taught in Lec06 and Lec07 of tiny.cc/robotics24. Watch these first — the ROS 2 code on the following pages will make a lot more sense once you've seen the plain-Python version of the same equations.

Lec06 — Differential Drive Forward Kinematics¶

The Euler integrator in ddrive's kinematics.py (euler_step()) is a direct port of the forward-kinematics model derived in Lec06: wheel velocities become a body-frame (v, omega), and the pose [x, y, theta] evolves by xdot = v*cos(theta), ydot = v*sin(theta), thetadot = omega. Watch this first if the plot-window motion in 2.3 ddrive doesn't feel intuitive — it's the same three equations, just running once per ROS 2 timer tick instead of once per for-loop iteration.

Lec07 — Inverse Kinematics of a Differential Drive Car¶

ddrive_ik's feedback_linearize() is the point-offset feedback-linearization control law derived in Lec07: a differential-drive robot can't move sideways, so you can't command an arbitrary Cartesian velocity at its center — but you can at a point P offset from the center by (px, py), by inverting the 2x2 matrix that maps [v, omega] to that point's Cartesian velocity. That inversion is exactly the a11, a12, a21, a22 block in feedback_linearize. The Kp*dt stability note on the ddrive_ik page isn't in the lecture — that's a discretization issue specific to porting continuous-time math into a fixed-dt ROS 2 timer.

ddrive_ik_msgs adds no new math on top of this — ResetPose and TrackTrajectory are ROS 2 plumbing (a service and an action) wrapped around this same tracking control law.
Applies to 2.4 ddrive_ik, 2.5 ddrive_ik_msgs
